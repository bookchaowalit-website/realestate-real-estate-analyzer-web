# Real Estate Analyzer — Yield Ledger

## Visual world

A property surveyor's annotated ledger: warm paper, blue measurement ink, and coral findings. The first viewport is an index of records and a yield line, not a generic finance dashboard.

## System

- Palette: paper `#f2ede3`, sheet `#fbf8f1`, ink `#20282b`, blue `#3b6b80`, coral `#b9523e`, sand `#dfd3bf`.
- Type: Georgia gives the record its editorial authority; Courier New is reserved for measurements and labels.
- Composition: property index beside one selected sheet; scenario strip closes the reading path.
- Signature interaction: selecting a record updates the yield bar and moving the down-payment rail recomputes cash and payback.
- Responsive rule: the index becomes a reading queue, then the selected sheet stacks its metrics vertically.

## Integrity

All listings and numbers are synthetic. The page explicitly discloses that it is not a listing feed, broker, or investment advice.

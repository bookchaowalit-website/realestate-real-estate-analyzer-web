# Real Estate Analyzer — Product Truth

Real Estate Analyzer is a browser-based decision tool for comparing sample property listings by price, rent, yield, and payback period. It is a synthetic planning demo, not a live property feed or financial advice.

## User and job

- Audience: a buyer or small investor comparing Bangkok-style rental opportunities.
- Primary job: enter a property scenario, see the yield calculation, and compare it against the sample district ledger.
- Mode: operate.

## Boundaries

- All listings and market figures are synthetic demonstration data.
- No broker, listing API, mortgage approval, or investment recommendation is claimed.
- The first release is client-side and does not require an account.
